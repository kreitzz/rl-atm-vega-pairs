What the Q-learner learns

Synthetic mean-reverting training; real equity evaluation

  • Recent changes become discrete states; actions buy, hold or sell under position restrictions.
  • Reward uses deviation from the true spread mean, minus transaction cost.
  • Synthetic paths supply that known mean; transfer to changing market regimes remains a question.
Explain state, action and reward briefly. The paper uses simulated paths to learn because its reward needs the true mean. Its definition of four move bins with a four-step lookback implies 256 combinations; a later statement of 16 states needs clarification.

Criticism → research design

What we need to test in the volatility extension

Longer evaluation, economically correct rewards and controlled sizing comparisons

  • One equity trading year does not establish robustness across regimes.
  • Formation and trading change together; isolate learned sizing with common proposals.
  • IV-spread convergence is not the same as profitable delta-hedged options.
  • Use contract marks, hedges, financing, costs and observable inputs.
The point is not that the paper is wrong. It gives us a useful framework, but options demand a different payoff and risk representation. This motivates our actual-contract simulator and fixed-size controls.

Retained trade

Approximately 30-day, 25-delta puts

P25 is downside volatility; neutrality is imposed at entry

  • Same option type; integer contract counts match initial vega within 10%.
  • Daily stock delta hedge; each leg held to expiry except audited corporate exits.
Contracts are selected at the previous close and checked on the next session. Execution deltas can drift. Vega neutrality does not equal gamma or jump neutrality, and vega is not continually rebalanced.

Economic state

Relative expected variance premium improves the state

27 existing inputs + three economic quantities and availability flags

VRP = IV² − estimated variance
estimated variance = 252 × EWMA20(r²)
  • Long-minus-short EWMA premium, prior-history premium z-score and realized/implied variance ratio.
  • 20-session half-life; historical normalization uses strictly prior 252 sessions.
  • Existing IV / RV, beta, correlation, DTE, VIX and profitability memory remain.
These are backward observable forecast proxies, not future RV. Missing features are neutral-filled and flagged. We have not separately proven this forecast dominates HAR or GARCH.

Learning mechanism

A contextual sizing policy optimized for daily Sharpe

Regression tree → discrete states → CEM size allocation

  • At most 8 leaves; actions 50 / 75 / 100%.
  • 50 search iterations × 192 candidate allocations; joint training P&L covariance.
  • Rolling 18-month completed-outcome training; monthly refits.
  • Direct contextual policy search—not Q-learning or a neural actor-critic.
Use precise terminology. In our project this sits under the RL research direction, but the retained model is contextual tree plus CEM. It has no within-trade Bellman updates. State-dependent sizing already changes portfolio admission and overlap.

Evidence and limitations

One Sharpe is a research finding, not a live guarantee

What the current experiment establishes—and what it does not

  • Learned sizing beats fixed sizing in this sample; current P25 has no fresh supervised-NN comparison.
  • 126-session reference bootstrap: Sharpe [0.137, 1.660]; best-fixed gap [0.008, 0.519].
  • Intervals are not selection-adjusted; repeated research and retrospective formation remain.
  • 2022 concentration, negative 2024, quote-quality and margin/assignment limitations.
Bootstrap intervals refer to the original research reference, not newly calculated ten-million intervals. The same-state profit-only policy is close at .979. We should claim adaptive sizing value, not universal RL superiority.

Conclusion and next step

Preserve the signal; test sequential exits separately

Equity relative-value framework → actual options → learned sizing

  • Finding: a downside-put relative-value signal with a persistent historical sizing benefit.
  • Next controlled test: identical entries / initial sizes; learn hold versus close both legs.
  • Use remaining life, moneyness, relative gamma and event / name inventory.
  • Then freeze the design and evaluate untouched dates with prospective pair formation.
The narrow scope is a strength: identify the economic signal first, then test whether a richer control policy adds value. Keep costs and executable marks common. Do not widen instruments, features and actions all at once.

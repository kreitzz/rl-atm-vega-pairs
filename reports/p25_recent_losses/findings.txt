UAL/AAL: long UAL options, short AAL options

initial vegas were $512.86 and $514.44 per volatility point, respectively.
Entry IV was 35.21% UAL and 38.51% AAL. Realized close-to-close volatility
over each actual entry-to-exit path was 22.95% and 53.56%, respectively.
The UAL leg lost $4,445 and AAL $22,551 after attributed trading costs, before
shared financing. Total -$26,997; May 29 alone -$24,326 in the standalone book.

AAL fell from $13.44 to $11.62 (-13.54%) on May 29, while UAL rose from
$50.64 to $51.83 (+2.35%). Held AAL put midpoint rose from $0.460 to $1.895;

ATM UAL/AAL also lost about $23,422 that day, through the short-call stock

American Airlines lowered its quarterly guidance on May 28, confirmed in its
8-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4515/000119312524148138/d800477d8k.htm

This event was not known at the earlier position entry; it cannot be used as
a retrospective trading veto. Vega matching at entry is not jump protection
or continuous vega/gamma neutrality throughout the holding period.

Why the screens and sizing did not protect the book
Signal-date snapshots from the actual common P25 feature cache are saved in
key_entry_signals.csv. UAL/AAL beta 0.858, correlation 0.796; UPST/COIN beta
0.862, correlation 0.511. Both met beta 0.85-1.15 and correlation >=0.5.
Their directed IV-spread ranks were in the lower quintile, and both had
positive 91-day and 365-day completed-trade profitability memory. RL selected
100% for these proposals. Several passed actual entry risk controls across
wings or adjacent dates. These observations establish that the existing
screens and allocation outputs admitted the losing exposures; they do not
prove a particular feature was causally responsible for the losses.

The reused P25 stream makes ATM and P25 entries related. Several losses are
the same underlying shock expressed in multiple wing/cohort positions.
The frozen sizing learner was not trained on sequential combined inventory.
Existing risk limits bounded exposure but did not eliminate shared jump risk.

Audit and limits
All eight option, stock, Greek exposure, netted execution/borrow and total
cash ledgers reconcile; largest daily reconstruction difference <$0.000001.
No modeled held option marks were used in these years. No crossed-quote
repairs occurred in the 30D books. Some observed quotes have no valid
European Black-Scholes fit and use prior-IV causal Greek fallback for
hedging; this matters especially near expiry and for American ITM options.
The dominant P25 jump-day quotes have valid fits. The accounting audit does
not independently certify vendor quote correctness or validate American
exercise/assignment treatment. The inspected top-loss quotes are saved in
worst_day_quotes.csv; fit flags are retained, not silently discarded.

Forward RV is retrospective diagnosis only, never a trading input. Its
legs.csv max_move_pct field is a percentage log return; the event moves
quoted above are ordinary close-to-close percentage returns. Simple RV is
not a full gamma-weighted options P&L decomposition.

The experiment uses already inspected history and inherited retrospective
roster/calendar conditioning, not a fresh holdout. No loss-based exclusions
were introduced. A justified next experiment would test shared underlying
jump/concentration sizing and better horizon-specific RV forecasts with
causal rolling evaluation, preserving the frozen benchmark.

ATM RV-IV CONTEXTUAL SIZING BANDIT

Inputs: trailing-year completed RV-minus-IV memory, signal-close ATM IV, and signal-close VIX.
Daily proposal pool: bottom 10% of past-year residual memory, identical across sizing policies. All entries short approximately 30-day ATM calls and delta hedge daily.
Sizing actions: 50%, 100%, 200% of the original $450-vega reference per trade. Account and execution assumptions unchanged.
Candidates compared on 2018-2021 validation: tree18, tree60.
Configuration and selection history are recorded in protocol.json; previous ladders remain in separate experiment folders.
Selected candidate: tree18. Monthly fits use only outcomes completed before month start; the selected procedure continues to refit during evaluation.
Training uses a bounded sample of up to twelve trade proposals at the first trading signal date of each month. This limits temporal/VIX coverage and is a prototype, not an exhaustive use of all training opportunities.
Unit actual-contract cash ledgers generate integer-count action payoffs; cash is separately replayed by action including financing. Funded capacity effects enter only during portfolio replay.
2018-2021 is called validation; 2022-August2025 is called test. These historical dates were examined in previous research, so this is not an untouched holdout.

Observed result: the selected bandit beats fixed 50% and 100% descriptively in both periods, but fixed 200% achieves the highest 2022-August2025 Sharpe (0.981) and full-period Sharpe (0.740). The bandit is strongest on validation (0.675), not on test (0.676). Its small test gain versus fixed 100% has a paired 95% interval of approximately [-0.293, +0.327]. These results do not establish a repeatable learning advantage.

Capital-aware sizing research, $10m equity.

The retained signal expert is unchanged: frozen monthly 18-month P25 variance-premium sizing models with50/75/100% allocations. A new sequential direct-policy-search overlay chooses0.5/1/2/4 times the expert size using prior-close reserve/stress/hedge utilization, name inventory, relative-to-cash drawdown, cash borrowing, and expert sizing. This is episodic derivative-free CEM policy search, not a neural actor-critic. Candidate policies are evaluated through their own actual shared cash, hedges, integer fills, admissions, and terminal runoff. No shadow standalone trade rewards are substituted for portfolio trajectories.

Raw-data coverage audit, 2018-01-01 through 2025-06-30.

All 256 requested 2010-2025 put partitions are present, with matching recorded
file sizes and parquet-footer row counts. No new data was downloaded. The
evaluation-period scan covers 128 partitions. Download hashes were retained
from metadata; the entire raw files were not rehashed.

Across the 345-pair roster, raw vendor-delta quote coverage near P25/P75 is
328,480/310,048 pair-days before OI/spread restrictions. At OI >=100 and relative
width <=25%, it falls to 136,653/56,791. At OI >=10 with the same width limit it
is 226,631/143,667. These raw diagnostics use vendor delta, not fitted BS delta.

The prepared dataset's fitted-IV presence exactly reconciles for every pair
and wing: P25 136,459 pair-days, P75 58,518. No post-selection IV-loss discrepancy
was found. This is coverage before beta, calendar, rank, memory and risk gates.

The current OI100/ATM-expiry pilot reproduces the prepared dataset exactly.
The pilot refitted 583,673 positive standard put quotes with OI >=1 and 21-45
calendar DTE; all pilot ATM expiry anchors lie within this range. No BS fitting
failures occurred among vendor near-target quotes in this pilot. A delta shift
can change bucket membership and is not equivalent to an IV fitting failure.

Conclusion: the main coverage losses are the OI100 selection threshold and
inheriting the ATM-call expiry, rather than an unfinished download. A common
selection protocol can choose the closest-to-30D usable expiry per delta bucket
and compare common OI thresholds without wing-specific exceptions.

These are signal-date quote-availability counts, not completed training labels,
actual trade counts, or profitability results. Newly admitted contracts still
need their holding histories, execution eligibility and risk checks before use.
All frozen policies and their backtest datasets remain unchanged. One worker,
one Arrow/BLAS thread, self-throttled 50% process CPU, 3 GiB RSS stop.

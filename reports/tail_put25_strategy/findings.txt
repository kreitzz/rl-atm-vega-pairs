Standalone 30D 25-delta put pairs — October 2, 2026

Moving from 10-delta to cached 25-delta puts improves the simple spread rule
substantially. The existing free-direction RL policy remains negative.
Everything except the contract bucket is held fixed: two spread inputs,
entry thresholds, optimizer, sizing ladder, gates, refit schedule, costs,
and capital/risk limits. The code/action-mapping comparison is tested.

The larger rule result remains concentrated. Full-period leaders are GPS/TJX

RL did not implement the rule as an overlay. Of its 43 full-book entries,
19 are in the rule's middle-rank flat region, nine go opposite to the raw
extreme-spread direction, and 15 agree. All nine opposite cases would fail
the profitability gate in the rule's intended direction; thus the gated
rule would skip them rather than automatically take the other side. Actual
rule eligibility agrees with only 15/43 RL signal dates. The RL fits trade
only AMD/MU, AMD/NVDA, BAC/JPM, BAC/C, BAC/WFC and C/WFC. Coverage improvement
helps trainability, but does not by itself make this learned policy profitable.
A fixed-direction skip/size overlay would be a different, untested design.

Rule execution: 129 accepted cohorts, 351 risk-limit entry rejections,
47 blocks while existing inventory is over limits, 46 unavailable entry
quotes, and one integer vega mismatch. RL: 43 entered, 85 risk-limit entry
rejections, 34 inventory-deleveraging blocks and six unavailable entry quotes.
Both include all transaction/funding costs. Rule option execution cost is
$13,406 (including $1,028 commissions); stock hedge costs are $19,887.

Exact design: same-side put/put pairs near 25% absolute delta and 30D expiry,
native delta tolerance 0.075, independently closest expiry on each name.
Initial vega mismatch at most 10%, daily delta hedging, own-expiry hold;
option vega is not rebalanced afterward. States are A-minus-B wing IV and its
252-session trailing rank, requiring 63 observed values. Missing pair states
are not forward-filled. Signal-close decisions execute next session.
Rule buys A against B at spread rank <=20%, sells it at rank >80%, otherwise
flat, at full size. Both use the same stock beta 0.85-1.15/correlation and
completed-outcome directional profitability gates. The rule has no fitted
validation gate. RL has a prior-year positive actual-inventory Sharpe gate,
monthly five-year completed-outcome refits, eight-leaf reference kernel,
seed 2026, and signed 25/50/75/100% sizing. No NN or larger action menu.
Training/validation commission hurdle is $0.65; actual execution is $0.03
per contract, midpoint +0.1 vol-point entry penalty, 1bp stock hedge costs,
and unchanged funding/borrow conventions. Limits are per pair sleeve, not
shared-name portfolio limits. Hold-to-expiry blocks additional risk during
breaches but does not force existing positions out.

Verified: nine relevant tests passed; 10,639 resolved scan cohorts in
feature-observed sleeves reproduce exactly before fitting. Both policies
have complete actual book accounting; none was dropped for unresolved P&L.
313 main inputs, 4,975 market files, all 2,661 frozen reference files and its
archive verified unchanged. Full run: 328 seconds, peak 713 MiB, one worker
at 50% of one core. Models/actions/ledgers are saved separately; no promotion.

This is inspected historical data, not untouched confirmation. Changing delta
also changes gamma/tail and execution economics; improved performance cannot
be attributed to quote coverage alone. European BS marks/settlement are used;
American early exercise/assignment is not modeled. No leverage conclusion.

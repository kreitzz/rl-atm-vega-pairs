Signal-mining decision, 2026-10-02

The frozen RL reference remains unchanged: annualized excess Sharpe 0.368 full history / 0.428 recent history. This study did not retrain policies or produce a new portfolio Sharpe.

Scope: 15 signal families, 25,872 resolved actual-contract pair cohorts across 126 pairs, with original current execution, hedge, borrow and financing costs. All 345 roster sleeves appear in coverage. Quarterly forecasts use completed historical outcomes; extreme thresholds use completed 2018-2021 observations. Later diagnostics cover 2022-Aug2025, an already-inspected period, not a fresh holdout.

The simple RV forecasts did not beat IV on MSE or QLIKE. Historical VRP normalization and market/industry residual VRP did not produce convincing trade signals. IV-factor reversion is the most consistent new rank-correlation lead across early and later periods, but later partial correlation controlling for existing states is only 0.031, 2025 was weak, and its positive tail payoff is concentrated.

Downside fraction (the proportion of trailing 21-session squared returns occurring on down days) has later partial rank correlation 0.047, but its standalone early-period relationship is weakly negative. It is more promising as a filter for the existing IV-spread direction than as a standalone trade signal.

Follow-up filter: use the original economic IV direction and a fixed absolute spread threshold of 15.177221 vol points; retain trades only when the cheaper-IV long leg also has the higher downside fraction. Compare on identical signal availability. No new threshold tuning or sign reversal.

The last comparison adds about$13,216 net excess P&L ($349,172->$362,388)
and improves the worst daily excess loss from$49,943 to$47,070. Its paired
126-session block-bootstrap Sharpe difference95% interval is[-0.078,+0.123].
The small Sharpe lift is not convincing evidence of improvement. Bootstrap
intervals do not remedy research selection, multiple comparisons, or the
absence of a fresh holdout. No model was promoted or frozen checkpoint
overwritten.

The inventory overlay is a fixed causal sizing adjustment, not a learned
sequential inventory policy. It uses the maximum gross vega already held
in either proposed name at the actual prior close, including other pairs
and wings. It reduces the learned size by1/sqrt(1+inventory units), rounded
to the same50/75/100 ladder, with a50% floor. It does not impose an absolute
aggregate exposure cap and cannot suppress an eligible signal entirely.

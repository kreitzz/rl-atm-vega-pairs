Deprioritize: put/call volume, open-interest changes, implied borrow, VVIX, equity news, trade news and litigation lack clear stable incremental relationships.
Late-filing apparent association rests on 53 overlapping entries in one pair, only 3 nonoverlapping observations, all in 2024. Restatements also have sparse, inconsistent outcomes.
Borrow: apparent -99.99 missing sentinel removed before the final screen; coverage falls substantially. Option-implied carry is not an actual stock-loan fee, and vendor methodology/historical vintages remain unverified.

Timing: daily market/activity data lag one business day; nearest-30D borrow lags one business day; events use the latest relevant record/filing timestamp plus one business day; public daily news has an extra 30-day lag; monthly news has a 45-day lag after month end. Extra lags do not certify historical data vintages.
Missing vendor event records are provisionally zero counts for linked issuers; absence does not prove comprehensive coverage.

Next candidate: add volume/open-interest gap and an availability flag to the unchanged RL baseline, with all costs and risk limits fixed. Keep pair selection unchanged so performance is attributable to the signal. No such fit has been run in this screen.

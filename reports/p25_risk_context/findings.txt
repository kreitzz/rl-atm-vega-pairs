Descriptive risk-context check: actual frozen30D P25 trades

194 accepted cohorts from2018 through partial2025 in the latest maturity
study, with actual realized trading costs. Complete cohort P&L before shared
financing. Values are not calendar portfolio returns or annualized Sharpes.
No new trades, filtering, retraining, threshold search or checkpoint changes.
All states are from the signal date, before the next-session fill.

Illustrative diagnostic thresholds: correlation<0.60; mean selected-put
IV>=60%; mean20-session RV / mean IV>=1.25. These are fixed round buckets,
not validated optimal entry or sizing thresholds.

Return correlation:
Below0.60:17 trades,5 pairs,15 winners; net+$108,049.
0.60-0.75:22 trades,12 pairs,11 winners; net+$19,196.
At least0.75:155 trades,32 pairs,91 winners; net+$105,200.
Thus a higher correlation cutoff would remove historically profitable
accepted opportunities as well as the losing UPST/COIN trade. This is an
observed-P&L attribution, not a simulated counterfactual NAV/Sharpe; altered
entry rules could change later risk eligibility, netting and financing.

Low correlation plus high IV:
7 trades,4 pairs,6 winners; net+$59,544. Six pre2024 trades made+$80,575;
the only2024-25 trade in that bucket was UPST/COIN, which lost$21,031.
This combination may indicate substantial risk but is not shown to lack
edge. Tiny, overlapping sample; the recent bucket is one known losing trade.

1<=RV/IV<1.25:35 trades,17 pairs; net+$17,975;60.0% winners.

Normalized by average initial per-leg vega, mean cohort P&L was+$5.68,
+$1.66 and-$0.42 per dollar of vol-point vega, respectively. Means are
equal weight across trades, not independent observations. The most elevated

Interpretation
The results support examining risk context in sizing. They do not justify
hard exclusion based on these thresholds from an already inspected history.
In particular, beta and correlation are not substitutes for jump exposure,
gamma exposure or shared underlying concentration across wings/cohorts.

RV greater than IV is direction-dependent: it can support a long-vol trade
and threaten a short-vol trade if recent RV persists. Mean pair RV/IV is a
blunt risk proxy, not a sign-correct relative-value forecast. A useful causal
state should retain long-leg and short-leg RV/IV separately, correlation x
IV, volatility mismatch and existing same-name inventory. The sizing learner
can distinguish expected return from downside-risk cost. Compare any later
change to the frozen policy using causal rolling training and unchanged
execution assumptions; report return, tail losses and trade coverage.

Limitations: accepted trades only, existing screen/calendar/entry selection;
historical cohort sizes learned by the frozen policy; overlapping holdings;
short recent period; inherited retrospective roster conditioning; no fresh
holdout. The RV/IV outcome comparison is exploratory signal diagnosis.

trade_context.csv and bucket_summary.csv contain all joined observations,
sample counts and normalized outcome distributions. Source hashes and
explicit limitations are preserved in audit.json.

Standalone 30D 10-delta put pairs: first strategy test, October 2, 2026

The simple spread rule is positive; the current two-feature RL policy fails
to improve it. Keep the frozen ATM checkpoint and this result separately.
No strategy stacking, new NN fitting, feature grid, or parameter search.

Concentration is substantial. The rule's leading full-period pairs are
UAL/AAL +$23,591 (7 entries), AMD/MU +$17,055 (4), and AMD/NVDA +$10,660 (3).
Replacing the largest winner with cash leaves +$13,331 and 0.160 Sharpe;
replacing all three leaves -$14,383 and -0.224 Sharpe. The recent equivalents
are 0.245 and -0.397. These replacements are ex-post diagnostics, not pair
selection rules. Positive total P&L does not establish a broad tail effect.

There are meaningful differences between the feature scan and this backtest.
Labels in the scan were independent overlapping opportunities without a
portfolio risk overlay. Here existing inventory, hedge leverage, stress loss,
reserve, and gross-vega entry limits are enforced on each actual pair sleeve.
The fixed full-size rule has 193 risk-limit entry rejections and 16 blocks
while existing inventory is over limits, versus 31 entered cohorts. RL has
39 and 14 respectively, versus 21 entered cohorts. Quotes also reject 29
rule proposals and three RL proposals. Risk is assessed before each entry;
the inherited hold-to-expiry mode blocks new entries during breaches and
does not force existing positions out. Cross-sleeve shared-name exposures
are not consolidated into a separate portfolio limit.

RL training is sparse. Of 4,183 monthly fit logs, 3,546 have fewer than 504
feature-complete training rows, 492 have no gated validation opportunity,
101 fail validation, and only 44 enabled monthly refits remain across six
pairs. Only two eventually get actual fills. The same 504-row minimum is
much harder to satisfy for sparse put-tail observations than for ATM calls.
The negative result is specific to this model and these controls; it does
not show that every RL implementation would fail on tail options.

Fixed design: actual approximately 30D puts near 0.10 absolute delta on both
legs; native cached delta tolerance 0.075; same option type and target bucket,
with independently closest expiries. Initial integer sizing matches leg vega
within 10%; options are not dynamically rebalanced for vega. Both legs are
delta hedged daily and each contract is held to its own expiry. No exits,
other tenors, calls, or other tail buckets are in the action menu.

RL sees exactly two inputs: A-minus-B put-wing IV and its rolling 252-session
historical rank, at least 63 observed values. Ranks use the observable pair
state calendar before eligibility or outcome filtering; absent states are
not forward-filled. Signal-close decisions execute on the next session.
RL uses the frozen reference eight-leaf tree partition and direct Sharpe
action optimizer, seed 2026, with flat and signed 25/50/75/100% sizes. It
refits monthly on five years of strictly completed outcomes; a prior-year
actual-inventory validation must have positive Sharpe before deployment.
Enabled monthly models and their thresholds/actions are saved as joblib.

The simple rule uses the same two observed states, buys A against B at
historical spread rank <=20%, sells A against B at rank >80%, and otherwise
stays flat, at full size. Both rule and RL share the same beta 0.85-1.15,
correlation, historical directional profitability gates and execution/risk
settings. The simple rule has no fitted policy or prior-year validation
gate. This comparison bundles RL sizing/learning/validation; it is not an
isolated proof of the effect of reinforcement learning or sizing alone.

Training/validation profitability hurdle: $0.65 per option contract, as in
the ATM reference; historical memory and directional gates use that hurdle.
Actual option commission: $0.03. Midpoint + 0.10 vol-point entry penalty,
1bp stock hedge costs, and funding/borrow conventions are unchanged. Current
option execution cost (including commission) is $3,366 rule / $716 RL over
the full period; stock hedge cost is $3,506 / $669.

Verification: seven tests passed for causal ranks, missing states, direction
gates, unchanged optimizer/action mapping and prior tail features. All 6,040
resolved scan cohorts in processed feature-observed sleeves reproduce
exactly at actual costs. Both models have complete accounting on the full
roster; no unresolved actual books were silently dropped. Strict outcome
cutoffs are checked in monthly logs. Verified 311 main inputs, 4,964 market
cache files and all 2,661 frozen reference files plus its archive unchanged.
The full run finished in 231 seconds, one worker at 50% of one core, peak
643 MiB RSS. The pilot used the same economics and was preserved on resume.

This history was already inspected and the put10 idea was selected using it;
none of these periods is an untouched confirmation sample. European BS
marks and settlement are used; American early exercise/assignment is not
modeled. The ATM reference has a different contract/state set and more
trades. The comparison uses identical evaluation dates and capital/cost
conventions, not matched individual trading opportunities.

Practical conclusion: retain the positive simple tail rule as an exploratory
standalone candidate, but do not promote or stack this RL policy. The first
issue to resolve before drawing conclusions about the learner is training
coverage; any smaller history minimum would be a new predeclared experiment,
not a correction to these reported results. Concentration and 2022 dominance
also need confirmation before calling the rule a reliable new trading stream.

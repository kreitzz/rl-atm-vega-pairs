ATM RV-minus-IV signal: calls versus actual matched straddles
===========================================================

These are exploratory historical comparisons on previously inspected dates.
All Sharpe ratios are annualized, using daily returns above the cash benchmark.
Validation: January 2018–December 2021. Later evaluation: January 2022–August 2025.

WHAT CHANGED

Retain all 35,774 original bottom-decile residual-memory signal proposals across
204 names. Replace each short ATM call with an equal-count short call and short
put on the same name, strike, expiry and adjustment factor. Delta hedge their
net stock exposure daily and hold until expiry under the original simulator.
The 100% budget is $450 of TOTAL straddle vega per vol point, not $450 per leg.
Straddles take directional short-vega exposure; they are not vega-neutral pairs.

Exact puts were recovered from already cached full chains. All original signal
rows matched: the earlier selected-contract-only 857-row pilot is superseded.
There are 854,093 cached quote rows, including 421,365 independently inverted
observed put quotes. No put prices were synthesized through put-call parity.
The original call controls were reused only after checking identical proposal
keys and execution settings.

Costs remain $0.03 per option contract plus a 0.10-vol-point adverse penalty
from observed mid, 1 bp stock execution, and 0.30% annual stock borrow. Positive
cash earns the reference rate; negative cash pays that rate plus 1%.
Cash dividends, declared-dividend information and the existing corporate-event
rules remain in the ledger. Early assignment and real pin execution are not
simulated; the existing intrinsic-cash-settlement convention is retained.

WHY THE FUNDED PORTFOLIOS DIFFER

For 2022–August 2025 at 100% size:
  Calls:    mean gross stock hedge $6.20m; mean reserve 29.9%; 928 entries.
  Straddle: mean gross stock hedge $2.75m; mean reserve 16.9%; 940 entries.

Only 610 entries are shared; 318 are call-only and 330 straddle-only. Therefore
this is a full portfolio comparison, not a common-trade causal attribution.
Daily excess-return correlation is 0.772.

The funding contribution relative to cash improves from approximately -$774k
for calls to +$61k for straddles, while option/hedge/dividend profit before fees
falls from $2.206m to $1.941m. All components reconcile to the daily ledger.
There are NO negative-cash days in either book: the call drag is foregone cash
interest from funding long stock hedges, not an additional margin-loan charge.

On frozen straddle100 positions, increasing annual stock borrow from 0.3% to
3% and 10% reduces later Sharpe from 0.990 to 0.934 and 0.790, respectively.
This includes cash-interest feedback but not re-admission or retraining.
Actual name-specific borrowing costs and executable fills remain unverified.

At straddle100 entries in the later period, independently fitted put IV exceeds
call IV by a median 0.210 vol points and a mean 0.481 vol points. The European
parity reference lies within the observed bid/ask conversion interval for 84.1%
of entries. This is a diagnostic, not an arbitrage test: American exercise and
stock borrow can change the relevant relationship. Together with admission
changes, observed quote differences also matter; the stock-funding attribution
alone is not a causal explanation of the performance difference.

RELATION TO THE RL ALLOCATION EXPERIMENTS

The earlier 1.859 later-period combination ALREADY contains learned put-pair
entry sizing. It combines that funded strategy with the fixed-200% call book
using a simple causal volatility-balancing rule. Calling it wholly non-RL
would be incorrect.

Holding this capital-allocation rule constant, replacing learned put-pair entry
sizes with the validation-preferred fixed-50% control reduces later Sharpe from
1.859 to 1.675. The paired block-bootstrap interval for that improvement crosses
zero. Validation scores are essentially tied (0.936 versus 0.936).

Additional portfolio-level learners have not beaten that simple rule on later
dates. The original allocation sweep evaluated 120 candidate procedures across
two call books; the vehicle-choice extension added 24 procedures. Inherited
direct allocation was also tested on each of the three straddle books. Their
results are retained, including failures; they are not evidence that capital
RL universally improves performance.

A retrospective fixed mix of calls200 and straddles200 reaches 1.869 later
Sharpe with the learned put-pair book. That is only 0.010 above 1.859 and was
not the validation winner; do not present it as a confirmed new advantage.

Straddles improve the standalone retained signal at 100% sizing, but replacing
calls with straddles does not improve its combination with put-pair trading:
later Sharpe is 1.561 for straddles100, 1.775 for straddles200 and 1.596 for
transferred straddle sizing, versus 1.859 for calls200. Diversification matters.

STRADDLE-SPECIFIC ENTRY TRAINING

The new learner keeps the inherited three inputs (completed call RV-minus-IV
memory, call IV and VIX), 18-month lookback, reward-tree states and 50/100/200%
ladder. It trains on straddle rewards, including integer contracts, both option
fees, net daily delta hedging, dividends, borrow and action-specific financing.
No additional hyperparameter sweep was conducted for this new entry learner.

Training samples use the first, sixth, eleventh and sixteenth calendar trading
session each month, with three evenly spaced current ranks on each date.
Sampling precedes reading payoffs. Of 1,080 sampled episodes, 518 supply valid
completed executable training labels. Missing labels do not remove any of the
35,774 daily funded proposals. No pre-2018 straddle labels are fabricated: use
100% sizing until 64 completed observations. The first nontrivial monthly fit
is November 2018; the last fit has 92 completed examples in its 18-month window.
All training outcomes must finish strictly before the monthly decision cutoff.

The new straddle-trained policy does not improve the result. Its validation /
later / full Sharpes are 0.633 / 0.608 / 0.621 versus 0.785 / 0.929 / 0.850 for
the transferred call-trained policy. Training history and sampling also differ,
so this is not a controlled test of reward target alone. It establishes that
this particular fresh straddle-training procedure failed, not that adaptive
straddle sizing cannot work.

The completed fixed50 control has validation / later / full Sharpes of
0.740 / 0.920 / 0.819, with 3,699 entries across the full period. The fresh
straddle-trained policy has 2,537 entries, the transferred policy 2,768, and
the fixed100 straddle policy 1,933. Conditional sizing changes admission.

Under the same causal volatility-balancing procedure with learned put-pair
entry sizing, later combined Sharpe is 1.609 for straddles50 and 1.514 for the
fresh straddle-trained policy. Neither exceeds the existing calls200 combination.
All controls, annual results and paired bootstrap intervals are saved in
../atm_p25_straddle_followup/. Charts are sizing_comparison.png and
growth_relative_to_cash.png in that directory.

The five follow-up straddle books independently reconcile daily option, stock,
dividend, financing, borrow and fee cash flows. The new unit-ledger scaling and
calendar-only sampling tests, together with all 14 memory-guard tests, pass.
All new research workers have exited; no continuing background run is required.

REPRODUCTION AND RESOURCE LIMITS

Code: scripts/research_atm_rv_straddle_full.py and associated buffered quote,
checkpoint replay, attribution, borrow-stress and sizing-followup modules.
Configuration: config/atm_rv_straddle_full.json and atm_rv_straddle_bandit.json.
Source manifests lock code/configuration and quote signatures. Funded books
save monthly cash/inventory checkpoints. Independent ledger and costless
put-call-parity tests check the implementation.

All numerical workers run sequentially at 20% of one CPU core, with hard 256 MiB
resident/physical-footprint limits and a 256 MiB system-swap-growth stop. Brave
is kept open. The allocation comparisons use fractional funded-strategy indices;
they are not a new joint option-and-stock inventory simulation.

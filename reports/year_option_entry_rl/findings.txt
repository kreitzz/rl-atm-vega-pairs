NATIVE ONE-YEAR BOOTSTRAPPED ENTRY Q

After management-only variants failed to improve the fixed hold baseline, allow RL to choose no entry or the original50 allocation using native long-tenor inputs. Candidate pairs/dates/directions remain the529 short-policy proposals; this is not a complete new one-year opportunity universe.
Entry Q target is observed first5-session block utility including .65 training opening commissions and unchanged spreads, plus discounted optimal weekly-management Q. Parent full-year64-fit critic is trained only on completed past weekly transitions. Entry label needs only its first weekly decision completed strictly before the refit, not future maturity payoff. Quarterly48-month entry fits;20 trees, depth3, leaf6. Fewer than16 completed entry labels or no parent critic keeps the original fixed50 entry.
Entry state is signal-close long-tenor carry forecast, slow carry/availability, long-versus-short term gap, native signed IV gap/level, return correlation and VIX. Greedy entry compares learned opening utility to the zero-reward no-position action. Greedy management uses the matching parent critic. No extra confidence rule, leverage, or changes to real .03 commission,0.1-vol-point option penalties and1bp stock costs.
Replay learned entries both with weekly management and fixed holding to separate entry selection from management. Entry proposal scores are identical within each pair of replays; realized fills can differ through shared risk limits and account equity. Local fractional shadow training approximates integer global book interactions.
This follow-up was chosen after earlier failures. All history is explored; confidence intervals are unadjusted for search, and2024–partial2025 is not an untouched holdout.

LONG-TENOR FITTED Q MANAGEMENT

Same 529 original pair/date/direction proposals. Fixed 50% entry on 180D/365D P25 contracts. The shared integer cash ledger, fees, daily hedges and all entry risk limits are unchanged.
Quarterly 48-month fits use past completed five-session transitions, including past portions of still-open contracts. Every transition end precedes its fit. No future expiry payoff is a training target. Cold start holds until 16 distinct cohorts and 640 action transitions.
State: current slow/recent expected carry, slow-RV availability, IV gap convergence, age fraction, relative delta and retained inventory. Actions: irreversible hold/half/close, checked weekly and filled next session only with executable actual quotes.
Natural runoff follows the fixed control and shadow labels: if one leg expires first, the remaining leg continues to its own expiry unless a prior learned paired exit has closed it. Entry vega neutrality is not continuously rebalanced. Earlier unaligned replay folders are retained for diagnosis and excluded from the final comparison.
Fitted Q iteration uses 20 shallow ExtraTrees, 16 Bellman updates and discount .98 per five sessions. Two utilities: expected step reward and a .5 standardized quadratic risk penalty. An adaptive follow-up after the initial 180D replay penalizes risk0 action advantage by one ensemble standard deviation; this discourages unstable exits and is not a calibrated confidence interval. Training option commissions remain .65 and actual .03; removes the former extra 2x elective-exit training penalty. Weekly shadow rewards use isolated fractional hedge/borrow costs; real evaluation is the reconciled shared-account integer replay.
This isolates sequential management from entry sizing. Additional opportunity selection, re-expansion, rolls, dynamic gamma/vega rebalancing and portfolio-level capital state are not part of this test. All history has been explored; no fresh holdout or multiple-comparison-adjusted significance claim.

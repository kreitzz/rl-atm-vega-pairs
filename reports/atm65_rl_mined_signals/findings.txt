New inputs are first-minus-second-leg gaps, with separate availability flags. Downside share is the trailing 21-session fraction of squared split-adjusted stock log returns on down days. IV-factor reversion is the negative trailing 21-session IV-change residual, standardized by own IV-change volatility; the leave-one-name-out broad IV factor and 252-session beta use original observable IV states and historical observations. Signal-close inputs execute next session. No payoff-based membership filtering.

110 pairs had usable downside observations on permitted trading dates; 63 had usable IV-factor observations. Uncovered variants retain the original policy. The combined variant was fitted on 110 pairs, with missing inputs zero-filled and flagged. All original employee inputs remain on their original sleeves. No NN was refitted.

Adding columns can also change tied tree partitions, so changes in fits that do not select a new value are not direct evidence of that value's effect.

The earlier mining result came from a fixed IV-spread direction-agreement filter, applied to standalone resolved cohorts. This experiment adds states to RL and lets the same existing tree/policy optimizer decide whether to use them; it does not impose that filter. In addition, some strong mining pairs such as SNAP/UBER and PLUG/BE frequently lack sufficient completed training observations for live policy fitting under the reference rules. The portfolio results therefore do not directly estimate the screened filter's portfolio Sharpe.

Next direct hypothesis, not executed in this experiment: specify a fixed downside direction-agreement rule for frozen reference recommendations and replay the same daily portfolio. Keep its costs, gates and sizing fixed. This would isolate the trade-filter idea before another model search.

All results remain exploratory because the signals were selected after inspecting this historical period. Paired calendar-quarter resampling gives 90% Sharpe-difference intervals including zero for all three variants, in both periods. This is descriptive uncertainty and does not correct for signal selection.

Executed-trade diagnostics of the frozen best RL checkpoint
==========================================================

Scope and accounting
The primary sample is the saved continuous 2018-August 2025 reference path:
224 entry cohorts, 29 traded pairs, 448 option legs. The reference annualized
excess Sharpe remains 0.368 full period and 0.428 for the separately restarted
recent period. This diagnostic does not refit a policy or modify the checkpoint.
The three latest feature variants are analyzed separately, not pooled as
independent observations. The full 345-pair roster remains intact.

Leg and cohort figures below include actual option execution fees/slippage,
allocated actual netted stock hedge execution costs and stock borrow. Shared
sleeve financing is kept separate. These are contributions to the actual paired
portfolio, not standalone executable strategies. pairs.csv and annual_pairs.csv
include shared financing. Idle-year dollar interest on prior profits is not a
new trade or a repeated successful signal.

Actual delta hedges, splits, option marks and expiry cash settlements are
reconstructed. Maximum daily portfolio accounting error is below $0.000001;
maximum cohort accounting error is below $0.000000001. The reference archive
and 2,661 checkpoint files were verified unchanged. The main pass took 46
seconds and peaked at about 643 MiB, under the one-worker resource guard.

What worked and failed
1. GES/TJX: short GES contributed +$69,916 across 19 legs (all profitable);
   long TJX contributed -$27,834. The paired trading contribution was +$42,082.
   These entries belong to only two overlapping episodes. TJX longs against
   AEO were profitable, so this is not evidence for a blanket TJX blacklist.

2. RCL/NCLH: three February 2023 entries, one episode, contributed -$17,965:
   long RCL -$13,905 and short NCLH -$4,060. Entry RCL IV was roughly 50-51
   versus subsequent RV of 39-43; NCLH IV was 55-60 versus RV of 59-61.
   Stock-return correlation was approximately 0.94. A comparatively lower-IV
   long can still be expensive, and high stock correlation did not rescue it.

3. THC/HCA: ten short-THC/long-HCA entries contributed -$17,606. Short THC
   lost $26,109 while long HCA gained $8,503. Losses clustered in May and
   October 2022; short THC IV of 45-57 was followed by RV of 62-77.
   In October THC's own-history VRP z-score was strongly negative despite
   its higher IV than HCA. However, negative own-history z also appeared in
   successful trades, so that observation does not validate a simple cutoff.

4. CENX/AA: long AA/short CENX earned +$14,979 in six entries across two
   episodes; the reverse direction lost $8,929 in four March 2025 entries
   in one episode. Each short CENX leg won; each reversed pair entry lost.
   In the losing direction the signal dollar-gamma-per-vega long/short ratio
   was approximately 0.70-0.73. Some long CENX legs lost despite forward RV
   exceeding entry IV. Exact contract path and hedge exposure matter.

5. HL/AG: the same direction gained $19,898 in a May-July 2022 episode and
   lost $12,645 in a January-March 2023 episode. Name identity alone does
   not define a permanently good or bad pair.

Across all legs, shorts contributed +$191,527 and longs -$64,056 before shared
financing. The profitable paired strategy depends heavily on short-vol income;
the long legs often impose a hedging cost. This does not establish that removing
the long hedges would improve risk-adjusted returns. Earlier single-leg policy
experiments underperformed the reference.

Concentration and reliability of memory
The 224 cohorts collapse into 59 connected within-pair holding episodes.
Episodes are still correlated across shared names and market events, not 59
fully independent samples. The top three pairs account for 42.3% of positive
pair net excess P&L. Nineteen winning short-GES entries are two episodes of
evidence, not nineteen independent confirmations.

Raw profitability memory was not monotonically helpful within the same pair.
For later entries its within-pair rank association with P&L per initial vega was
-0.07 for 91-day memory and -0.13 for 365-day memory in the follow-up analysis.
These associations are conditional on executed trades, not causal effects.
Overlapping outcomes and stale regimes are hypotheses to test, not proven
explanations. Full-size entries averaged $1.52 net P&L per initial vega versus
$2.95 at half size. The action ladder optimizes an objective; it is not a
calibrated probability of a winning trade. These groups have different trades
and regimes, so they do not establish that imposing a half-size cap helps.

Entry signals with more interesting descriptive evidence
The following correlations rank signal and realized net P&L/initial vega within
each pair, then combine pairs with equal weight. No new policy is fitted.

                                      pre-2022 completed     later entries
Short IV minus long IV                      +0.203                +0.223
Long/short dollar-gamma per vega ratio       +0.237                +0.210
IV-change correlation versus own history    +0.092                +0.188
IV/stock-correlation wedge versus history   +0.095                +0.117

The early sample contains only 45 completed entries; the later sample has 178
entries, with one boundary-spanning cohort in neither split. Six pairs qualify
for the early within-pair correlations above and fourteen for the later ones.
The history-normalized correlation variants were proposed after examining this
history. These are exploratory associations, not fresh holdout validation or
evidence of improved portfolio Sharpe. Gamma geometry is mechanically related
to IV and tenor, so its incremental information also remains unproven.

Stock beta closeness alone barely distinguished winners from losers: median
absolute distance from one was 0.083 versus 0.086, within the already selected
0.85-1.15 range. The promising IV-change result describes a pair becoming more
correlated than usual; it does not say that the highest absolute-correlation
pairs are best. No clear general entry filter emerged from earnings-inside-
contract, short RV above IV, or short own-history VRP z alone. The previous
downside-direction mining rule also did not carry over to the actual reference
RL entries: later agreeing trades had lower mean per-vega outcomes than
disagreeing trades. A new downside filter is therefore not supported here.

RV proxy versus actual option outcomes
Using holding trading sessions and quoted calendar tenor to align cumulative
realized variance and implied variance, their simple profit-sign proxy disagrees
with actual gross option-plus-stock-hedge-and-dividend P&L on 73 of 448 legs
(16.3%). This comparison is before execution costs or shared financing.
Changing gamma/moneyness, jumps, discrete hedging and rate/dividend effects can
contribute; this test does not isolate their individual contributions. Forward
RV and holding price jumps here are retrospective explanations, never inputs.
The existing RL reward already uses actual simulated contract P&L.

Signal-close contract IV matched the policy states exactly for all 224 entries.
Mean signal-to-next-session change in short-minus-long IV spread was -0.07
volatility points. Some individual changes were large, but broad systematic
erosion of the entry spread is not established as the main explanation.

Why the recent variants lost performance
Relative to baseline executed trades, adding both mined states dropped 40
entries that had earned $36,109 and added 47 that lost $9,559. The retained
184 entries contributed $91,362 in baseline and $95,280 in the variant.
Downside alone dropped 35 entries worth $49,439 and added 51 worth only $6,278.
This points mainly to changed trade selection, not universally worse outcomes
on the retained entries. It is retrospective attribution, not permission to
select winners using their realized payoffs.

Prioritized next experiments, not implemented in this diagnostic
1. Add separate long/short name profitability histories, built only from
   previously completed outcomes and deduplicated asset/date/contract records,
   with availability and effective-history/uncertainty states. Measure distinct
   episodes and recent deterioration rather than treating overlapping entries
   as repeated independent confirmation. Preserve the paired risk limits.
2. Test the history-normalized IV-change-correlation state and dollar-gamma-
   per-vega geometry separately, against existing IV and tenor features, with
   fixed costs and walk-forward settings. Do not infer thresholds from these
   executed winners and losers or add all candidate states simultaneously.

These ideas require new walk-forward validation. This report does not establish
fundamental economic causes for the named stocks, blacklist names, select a new
winner, change costs, or modify the frozen reference.

INTERPRETATION

30D European-parity-Greek control, 64 identical cohorts/counts: before execution and stock borrowing, after financing, puts earn $64,594.97 and calls $65,259.63. Net excess P&L is $53,082.77 versus $46,491.48; extra call hedge trading and stock borrowing explain most of this endpoint difference. Daily excess Sharpe is .4314 versus .2405. Monthly return correlation is 0.9719, compared with .4695 daily. This supports economically similar 30D payoff exposure with additional call costs and short-lived mark/carry variation, rather than evidence of a completely different volatility signal. It does not separately identify stale quotes versus changing carry. Sampling diagnostics retain the daily Sharpe as the primary measure; monthly Sharpe is not substituted to optimize results.

This control is a 64-trade subset through the common July 2024 entry cutoff, not the full ~1.0 Sharpe put checkpoint. Native independently fitted call Greeks use 60 matched trades; their puts/calls are .4010/.1112. Longer maturities show larger carry and model differences. European declared-dividend-only fits, American exercise/assignment omissions, hard-to-borrow pricing and floating cash financing versus term discounting require further validation before attributing a long-expiry put/call difference to alpha.

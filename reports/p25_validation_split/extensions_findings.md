# Extensions on the revised date split

Choices use2018–2021 validation only, then frozen configurations are evaluated over2022–29August2025. Existing causal monthly fits and continuing books are reused; no new quote loading, refitting or ledger replay.2025 is partial.

These are separate strategy experiments: the30D exit studies retain the original33-input entries and do not manage the newly selected27-input policy. Locked Q entries are attribution controls. Longer-tenor signals and samples differ. The native365D comparison uses the corrected return-reconciled_v2 histories, replacing the stale original numbers.

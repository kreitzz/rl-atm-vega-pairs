Trade-quality RL feature tests on the frozen reference
=====================================================

None of the three separately tested additions improved annualized excess Sharpe.

                             Full 2018-Aug2025   Recent 2022-Aug2025
Frozen reference                   0.368039             0.427591
Blocked name profitability         0.010578            -0.037766
IV-correlation history regime      0.262443             0.286153
Dollar-gamma/vega geometry          0.348137             0.422870

Both periods use sqrt(252) daily excess-return Sharpe above the cash benchmark.
Recent is a separate flat-start replay, not a slice of the continuous full path.
All 345 fixed sleeves are retained, with no unresolved accounting or exclusions.
Full-period entries: reference 224; name memory 215; IV regime 209; gamma 226.
Full-period active pairs: reference 29; name memory 31; IV regime 30; gamma 29.

Experiment specification
Each variant adds only its own group to the existing reference inputs. Employee
leadership inputs remain on the original 28 covered sleeves. Costs, inventory
limits, historical pair screens, beta/correlation and profitability gates,
monthly refits, completed-outcome purging, prior-year validation, 8-leaf tree,
seed 2026 and Sharpe sizing optimizer are identical to the reference. Training
commission is $0.65; actual commission is $0.03, plus the same 0.1-vol-point
midpoint entry penalty and stock hedge/borrow/funding costs. ATM30 calls remain
held to their own expiries and delta hedged daily, with initial vega matching.
No NN training, joint variant, threshold search or reference promotion.

Name memory: distinct hypothetical asset/date/contract outcomes from the cached
single-name ATM30 simulations at actual $0.03 costs, including standalone
financing. These are historical predictors, not attribution of actual paired
positions. Nonoverlapping intervals are selected by their known entry/expiry
schedules before inspecting payoff values; a missing payoff still occupies its
interval. Only outcomes strictly completed before the signal session enter the
preceding 365-calendar-day window. Separate long and short means, dispersion
divided by sqrt(count), block count and availability are provided for each name.
Means/uncertainty require at least four resolved blocks. That dispersion measure
is an uncertainty proxy, not a formal independent-sample standard error.
There are 19,929 sampled intervals and 18,727 resolved samples across the history.

IV regime: 126-session correlation of daily changes in the two IV series,
normalized against its own preceding 252-session mean and standard deviation,
with minimum 63/126 observations and a separate availability flag.

Gamma geometry: log ratio of the signal contracts' BS dollar gamma per quoted
vega, using observed spot, IV, tenor, strike, rate and known dividend adjustment,
plus availability. No new Black-Scholes fit. A/B ordering is fixed; the policy
still chooses direction and size. Existing IV and tenor inputs are retained.

What changed in the trades
The name-memory variant improved several known losing pairs but damaged larger
winners. All following figures are full-book net excess dollars, including
shared financing, rather than allocated leg contributions:

Pair             Reference P&L     Name-memory P&L      Reference/new entries
GES/TJX             +47,795              +2,473                 19 / 8
HL/AG               +13,626             -21,429                 24 / 28
AU/NEM              +20,870                   0                  6 / 0
KBH/TOL              +7,704              -8,729                  8 / 7
THC/HCA             -19,539              -6,475                 13 / 7
OIH/HAL              +1,805             +16,565                 13 / 7

GES/TJX lost all its 2022 entries in the memory variant. Two retained March
2023 entries were reduced from full to quarter size; the other entries changed.
HL/AG retained the long-HL/short-AG direction but added more January-February
2023 entries, despite reducing size on some retained entries. This does not
identify an individual memory column as the causal source of the deterioration.
The whole feature group was added together within this variant.

Memory's 2023 annual Sharpe fell from +0.815 to -0.939. Its 2025 Sharpe improved
from -0.976 to -0.121, but that did not offset the lost performance elsewhere.
The overall result is not explained by simply avoiding trades: 215 versus 224
entries is a modest count change, while directions, dates and sizes changed.

The IV-regime variant improved CENX/AA from +$5,279 to +$26,782 and avoided all
four March 2025 long-CENX/short-AA entries. It also changed earlier 2024 entries,
so the entire pair improvement cannot be attributed solely to the avoided loss.
However, OKE/KMI fell from +$18,962 to -$1,626; the variant added nine losing
HAL/SLB entries worth -$12,218 and lost the two profitable AEO/TJX entries.
Annual 2025 Sharpe improved to -0.376, while 2023 fell from +0.815 to +0.093.

Gamma geometry increased full-book net excess P&L from $145,778 to $156,459,
but annualized excess-return volatility rose about 13.5%, versus about 7.3%
growth in mean excess return. Therefore Sharpe fell slightly. A newly traded
PENN/CZR cluster of four October 2024 entries contributed +$28,443; other pairs
gave up profits. CENX/AA entries and 2025 annual Sharpe were essentially unchanged.
This did not solve the identified 2025 loss regime.

Actual new-feature usage in enabled refits
Name memory: new values used in 352/493 enabled refits (71.4%).
IV regime: new value used in 208/520 enabled refits (40.0%).
Gamma geometry: new value used in 54/532 enabled refits (10.2%).
Column addition can also change tied tree partitions. A changed recommendation
does not alone prove that a new value drove the decision in that specific fit.

Verification and interpretation
Eight relevant unit tests passed. The audit reconstructed all 139 panels,
verified 465 source files, recovered 4,867 monthly baseline controls and verified
the frozen archive plus 2,661 checkpoint files. Original input columns and
training costs/risk rules remain unchanged. Every executed variant entry had
the new inputs available. Sampling stayed identical with all payoff labels
erased; selected within-name holding intervals were verified nonoverlapping.
Training/replay took about 25 minutes; peak RSS was 668 MiB, under the single-
worker 50%-of-one-core duty cycle and 3 GiB memory stop.

Paired quarter-block resampling gives these descriptive 90% intervals for
full-period Sharpe differences versus reference:
  Name memory: -0.357, interval [-0.628, -0.075].
  IV regime:   -0.106, interval [-0.346, +0.116].
  Gamma:       -0.020, interval [-0.126, +0.085].
These do not correct for choosing hypotheses after inspecting this history.
The early/late descriptive rank correlations motivated experiments, but they
did not demonstrate incremental entry-policy value. The current implementation
of name memory is clearly unsuccessful in this comparison. The other two
variants do not provide evidence to replace the existing reference.

The frozen 0.368/0.428 reference remains unchanged. Candidate actions, trade
ledgers, daily NAVs and monthly fits are saved for inspection. No commit/push
or new checkpoint promotion was performed in this experiment.

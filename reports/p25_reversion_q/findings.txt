Retained 30D 25-delta puts; all 36,327 cached candidate states across 125 pairs and 161 assets. Evaluation candidate universe has 17,967 pair/date states. Original beta .85–1.15, correlation >=.5, and retrospective formation context retained. Old rank and profitability entry vetoes removed. Missing future paths exclude training rewards only, never evaluation candidates. Matched runs retain all original 608 proposals.

Four recent IV-spread moves, binned by strictly prior change volatility, encode 256 patterns plus missing history. Add trailing relative-spread valuation and an empirical excursion-reversion bucket. Excursions use a prior mean and 2-SD threshold; completed, failed at 63 sessions, and ongoing/censored excursions are explicit. This causal excursion diagnostic is not an exact replication of the paper local-extreme EMRT estimator. No hedge-coefficient search; actual quantities remain entry-vega matched. Selected-bucket spread paths are signals; held contracts retain their own actual price paths, strikes and expiries.

Monthly 18M fits require scheduled expiry strictly before fit; minimum 32 completed cohorts. Pooled tabular fitted Q, 40 Bellman iterations, gamma .99 per daily step. Held state includes age, direction, and initial size. Actions hold or paired close; entry chooses flat or either direction, at 100% for fixed or 50/75/100% for sized. Flat has zero episodic value; no modeled continuation into a future new trade. Actual historical daily option/hedge PnL shadow labels, not an oracle equilibrium reward or synthetic OU paths. Initial declared mean/variance utility r-.25*r*r/(2*training-only reward std), a risk proxy rather than exact Sharpe. Follow-up risk0 uses plain actual PnL to test whether the added utility penalty suppresses entries. A further management-only control preserves all original entry directions and learned sizes. Its locked-inventory attribution control retains the original 212 accepted dates and initial integer counts, still subject to risk checks; those original execution counts are a counterfactual control, not a live predictor. Final model version3 includes failed exit attempts as continuing held transitions and unfilled integer-size entries as zero-exposure outcomes, rather than conditioning entry estimates on successful fills. Final execution version3 avoids an elective order when a known leg expiry would precede its next-session fill, matching the paired shadow-close transition. Initial executable-only close estimates and their replays are retained separately as preliminary diagnostics. Categorical pooling handles sparse patterns without activating/deactivating individual pairs. Cold start uses the original extreme-rank rule at full size and holds; no profitability veto.

Daily management decides after close, fills next session only with executable quotes. Both legs close together when both survive. Unequal expiries retain original own-expiry runoff; no continuous vega rebalancing, reversals of existing inventory, trims or rolls. Independent fixed-integer shadow cohorts approximate training; final evaluation is the existing reconciled shared-cash/shared-stock-hedge account with NAV-scaled integer counts and unchanged original risk limits. Option commission .65 in training, .03 in evaluation; .1 vol point entry and elective exit penalty, stock hedging 1bp, floating cash and original borrow/dividend conventions.

$10m equity, Jan2018–Aug2025, 2025 partial. Annualized daily cash-excess Sharpe, sqrt252. All evaluation dates already explored; no untouched holdout. Sequential mean-reversion timing is exploratory and cannot be described as an improvement until the matched and broad comparisons support it. Previous checkpoints preserved.

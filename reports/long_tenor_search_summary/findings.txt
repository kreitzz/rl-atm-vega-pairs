LONG-EXPIRY RL POLICY SEARCH

Compare same frozen pair/date/direction proposals and actual matched-type contracts, shared cash, daily delta hedge, entry vega neutrality and unchanged execution/risk conventions. Every native entry proposal remains an explicit decision. P25 and same-strike approximately C75 trained separately.
Three declared search families: (1) 24/48-month quarterly native entry/exit fits using original executed cohorts and four-leaf policies; (2) all remaining native executable opportunities independently labeled, 252-session EWMA variance forecast, two-leaf regularization and 2x training elective-option-exit penalty; (3) same broad labels/signals with four leaves and learned entry actions 0/25/50/75/100%, plus identical learned-skip fixed-size controls. Management always irreversible hold/half/close, weekly checks and next-session actual observed fills.
Training uses only fully completed original scheduled expiries, minimum16 episodes and original cold-start sizes/hold. Compact episodic direct policy optimization learns action maps from realized rewards; not neural Q-learning. Shadow training uses fractional unnetted hedge/borrow costs; all final single-tenor tests execute integer quantities and reconcile independent cash flows.
Full-history strongest variants/allocations are hyperoptimized exploratory results. A separate 2018-2023-selected comparison shows 2024-partial2025 behavior, but those dates were previously inspected and are not a fresh holdout. Bootstrap intervals do not adjust for this search. Saved-return portfolio blends include component costs, but not joint hedge netting or shared admission/integer allocations. American exercise/assignment and long-term expected-dividend/BS hedge-model limitations remain, especially for deep calls.

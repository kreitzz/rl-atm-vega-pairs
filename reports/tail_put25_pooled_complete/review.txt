Pooled tail sizing: completed first pass

Coverage
All 345 original candidate pairs were assessed from cached option data. All
136,459 observed evaluation states across 332 quote-covered pairs received
the pooled model, including 193 pairs absent from inherited ATM state panels.
The other 13 pairs lack usable overlapping evaluation quotes. All 90 monthly
models were enabled; there were no pair validation or profitability vetoes.
The core spread signal still defines direction and the requested monthly beta
0.85-1.15/correlation >=0.5 screen still determines executable pair candidates.
The model uses 15 numeric signals plus 12 missing-value flags and can size
0/25/50/75/100%. Optional feature availability never excludes a quoted state.

entries across 106 pairs, $528,209.95 net excess P&L.
Pooled RL sizing: annualized cash-excess Sharpe 0.257615, 1,015 accepted

For the continuing book from 2022, Sharpe is -0.220771 and -0.169506,

Interpretation
Coverage is repaired; this first pooled sizing model does not improve Sharpe.
Both new books rely heavily on 2020. Removing the RL book's three largest
winners leaves Sharpe 0.026219. This is previously inspected history, not a
fresh holdout or proof of a stable broad-universe effect.

Accounting and risk
All 690 pair/model books reconcile and finish flat, including repaired books.
Known nonstandard corporate events are closed as complete pairs before the
event at observed quotes, with execution costs and commissions. Other trades
hold to their own expiry. The two models have five modeled held marks in total
(three control, two RL), using strictly causal inputs; these never supply
synthetic entry quotes. COP declared supplemental USD cash dividends enter
both option dividend PV and stock-hedge cash accounting. LEN's 2019 stock
adjustment-factor anomaly is repaired in a working copy; raw prices and all
original cached data remain unchanged. The policies were frozen before repair.
These assumptions and their primary source links are in protocol.json.

Limits apply at entry. Holding to expiry can subsequently breach them; this
occurred on 5,261 pair-sessions for the control and 726 for RL, summed across
independent pair books. Thus this is not a portfolio with enforced continuous
risk limits. Pair capital/risks remain separate; cross-pair inventory is not
netted. Early exercise and assignment are not modeled.

Verification
Nine focused tests passed, including ordinary-book parity with the reference
engine, paid whole-pair corporate-action exits, causal missing held marks,
no synthetic entry quotes, missing-feature coverage, training purging and
shared daily P&L aggregation. Frozen checkpoint hashes were verified.
The main run took 720 seconds at 50% of one core and peaked at 568 MiB.

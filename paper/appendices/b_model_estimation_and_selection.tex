\subsection{Model Selection and Research Chronology}\label{app:feature-selection}\label{sec:10}\label{app:research-chronology}

The put-sizing specification was selected from 33 archived screened configurations ranked by 2018--2021 validation excess Sharpe within the established 30-day, 25-delta design and pair roster. The grid varied features, training windows and action menus. Baseline, IV-factor and term-structure scores tie exactly at 0.575; alphabetical configuration ordering retains the baseline. The selected policy uses 27 inputs, an 18-month window and 50/75/100\% sizes. Selection used a \$345 million research account; the funded comparisons use \$10 million.

The straddle's 48- and 60-month windows tie at validation Sharpe 0.775; their later Sharpes are 0.960 and 1.041. \textbf{Verification pending:} an earlier draft attributes refinement to these later-period results. Confirm whether and when this occurred against experiment records and author notes; the reported scores alone do not establish the development chronology.

Table~\ref{tab:q-reward-preferences} reports four illustrative Q preferences alongside the original validation-selected \(\lambda=0.025\) policy at \(\gamma=0.99\). Its validation and later Sharpes are 1.437 and 0.282; the illustrative settings do not replace that selection. Supporting materials retain the original selection record and full results (Section~\ref{app:reproduction}); chronological fitting does not establish an untouched evaluation.

\subsection{Statistical Inference}\label{app:statistical-inference}

The paired interval for the put-sizing Sharpe difference uses 1,000 circular block-bootstrap resamples with 126-session blocks, holding the compared policies fixed. The interval conditions on the retained policies and historical paths; it does not adjust for model or research selection. The estimated gain and interval are reported in Section~\ref{sec:put-results}. Neither this interval nor chronological fitting measures the bias from development choices or establishes prospective performance.
